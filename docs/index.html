    Scraping policy

    Public article listing pages only. Government sites, login-walled content and paywalled content are refused in code, not worked around: nothing here attempts to authenticate, bypass a login, or reuse a session cookie. Pages about private individuals are out of scope, and author bylines are dropped at extraction time.

    A site returning HTTP 403 to a plain request is not refused. Several legitimate sources block naive clients and work correctly through Bright Data's unblocking layer. Being blocked as a bot is a transport problem; being behind a login is a permission boundary, and we stop at the boundary.
